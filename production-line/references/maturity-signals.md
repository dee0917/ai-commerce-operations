# Maturity Signals Checklist

> **Purpose:** A site that looks AI-generated has none of these. A site that looks
> like it's been running for 5 years has ALL of them. These are the details that
> separate a real store from a template.

## Required Maturity Signals (must implement ≥ 12 of 21)

### Header & Navigation (pick ≥ 3)
- [ ] Announcement bar with rotating messages ("Free shipping over $75" / "New: Summer Collection")
- [ ] Currency/language selector (even if non-functional, the UI element matters)
- [ ] "Account" with dropdown (not just a page link)
- [ ] Search with autocomplete suggestions (showing product names)
- [ ] Mobile hamburger with categorized menu (not just page links)
- [ ] Cart icon with item count badge (animated on add)

### Social Proof (pick ≥ 3)
- [ ] Customer review count on product cards (e.g., "4.8 ★ (142)")
- [ ] "As Seen In" press logo bar — ONLY with real, authorised press coverage. Fabricated or "generic" press logos breach UK DMCCA / CPRs. No real coverage -> omit this signal.
- [ ] Customer photo gallery / UGC section
- [ ] Testimonial carousel with names + locations
- [ ] "X people are viewing this" or "Sold 500+ times" — ONLY when wired to real analytics / real sales records. No real data -> omit (do NOT fabricate a number).

### Trust & Credibility (pick ≥ 3)
- [ ] SSL/payment method badges in footer (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal icons)
- [ ] "30-Day Returns" or "Free Returns" badge near Add to Cart
- [ ] Shipping estimator ("Arrives by March 28")
- [ ] "Secure Checkout" messaging on checkout page
- [ ] Physical address or "Contact Us" with form

### Content Depth (pick ≥ 3)
- [ ] Blog/Journal/Stories section with ≥ 3 article previews
- [ ] "About Us" with team/founder story (not lorem ipsum)
- [ ] Brand values / mission section with icons
- [ ] Size guide or product specifications table
- [ ] Ingredient/material sourcing information

## Forbidden AI Tells

These patterns instantly reveal a site is AI-generated. NEVER do these:

- ❌ Lorem ipsum or placeholder text anywhere
- ❌ "Welcome to [Brand]" as hero headline (real brands don't do this)
- ❌ Generic "Shop Now" as the only CTA text (use specific: "Explore the Collection", "Find Your Scent")
- ❌ All product descriptions same length and structure
- ❌ Perfect symmetry everywhere (real sites have intentional asymmetry)
- ❌ Missing 404 page or error states
- ❌ No favicon
- ❌ Default browser scrollbar (should be styled or hidden)
- ❌ Footer with only links (needs newsletter, social, payment icons)
- ❌ Every product same price format (mix $29, $29.99, $30.00)
- ❌ No sale/clearance items (real stores always have some on sale)
- ❌ Navigation items all same character length
- ❌ Mixed aspect ratios inside a single product grid. One grid uses ONE aspect ratio; vary composition and camera distance, not the ratio.（2026-08 五站實測：gridwell 商品格 239×208、aurazen 商品 302×302 與分類卡 292×389 兩個格線各自統一、vanguard 全部 292×292、inkandecho 全部 296×296，受肯定的站全部同格線同圖比）
